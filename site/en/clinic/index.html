How is patient face data handled under PDPA?

The simulation and landmark detection are computed on the clinic’s computer; the scan is not sent anywhere to be processed. The clinic’s system keeps a scan sent from a phone only for a patient whose consent is recorded. Each clinic’s data is kept apart, and the system logs who opened or changed a case. Obtaining consent and telling patients the purpose remain the clinic’s duties under PDPA.

2During the consultation

Consult with Faceliora Clinic

Use desktop models for discussion, not treatment prediction.

3D facial simulation software that clinicians and clinic teams use with the person being consulted. Every view is a simulation for communication, not a guaranteed result.

  • Injection simulation: filler, toxin and fatChoose HA filler, botulinum toxin or fat and press the face where it goes. Amounts are held to each area’s limits, and the change in shape follows published measurements of volume per cc.
  • Surgery simulation across 13 facial zonesNose, chin, jaw, cheeks, forehead, temples, under-eye, lips, eyes, brows, sub-brow, nasolabial folds and under the chin, adjusted with sliders in millimetres, on both sides or one, in layers you can show, hide and compare.
  • Thread-lift simulationJawline, under-chin, cheek, nasolabial fold, mouth corner, brow and eye-tail threads. The simulation lifts only skin the scan shows hanging, so a face with little laxity changes little.
  • Skin analysis from the 3D scanSpots, red spots, pores, blackheads, wrinkles, redness and melasma-like pigment found on the scan, shown in simulated daylight, red and UV views for a clinician’s review. Not a diagnosis.

Capability details are as described by the previous website; not yet verified by product testing.

How accurate is the simulation?

It is computed from the shape of the skin in the client’s scan, and injection effects follow values measured in published studies, which describe groups, not a prediction for one person. A scan sees only the outer skin, not muscle, fat or bone, so real results can differ. Every view and export carries a simulation watermark, and medical figures in documents are shown only after a clinician confirms the scale.

What can the client take home?

A consultation sheet as a PDF with before-and-after images from the 3D model and a comparison video. The quotation is included in the PDF once the clinic has entered every price and checked it against the plan. Every file carries the simulation watermark and promises no result.

What the simulation cannot show

  • Simulation is not diagnosis, prediction or a guarantee of treatment outcomes.

What is Faceliora Clinic?

Faceliora Clinic is 3D facial simulation software for aesthetic clinics. On the client’s own facial scan it simulates filler, toxin, fat, surgery and thread lifts, analyses the skin and summarises the consultation in a sheet. Every view is a simulation for communication, not a guaranteed result, and never replaces a clinician’s examination.
