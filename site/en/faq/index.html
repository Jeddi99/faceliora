General

Which product scans?

Faceliora Scan scans on iPhone. Faceliora Clinic uses supplied models on desktop. Faceliora App is a separate personal app.

Link to this question
Can simulation predict treatment results?

No. It supports discussion, not diagnosis, prediction or guaranteed outcomes. A doctor must assess treatment.

Link to this question
How accurate is the simulation?

It is computed from the shape of the skin in the client’s scan, and injection effects follow values measured in published studies, which describe groups, not a prediction for one person. A scan sees only the outer skin, not muscle, fat or bone, so real results can differ. Every view and export carries a simulation watermark, and medical figures in documents are shown only after a clinician confirms the scale.

How is Faceliora Web different from Faceliora Clinic?

Faceliora Web is used on the web by anyone and works with 2D images. Faceliora Clinic is software for clinics that simulates injections, surgery and thread lifts on a 3D facial scan and summarises the consultation.

For clinics

What is Faceliora Clinic?

Faceliora Clinic is 3D facial simulation software for aesthetic clinics. On the client’s own facial scan it simulates filler, toxin, fat, surgery and thread lifts, analyses the skin and summarises the consultation in a sheet. Every view is a simulation for communication, not a guaranteed result, and never replaces a clinician’s examination.

Does Faceliora Clinic create its own 3D scans?

Faceliora Clinic does not scan. Scan the face with the Faceliora Scan app on an iPhone and send it to Faceliora Clinic, or import a .glb, .obj or .zip file from another scanning app. Confirm supported iPhone models with our team first.

Data and privacy

How is patient face data handled under PDPA?

The simulation and landmark detection are computed on the clinic’s computer; the scan is not sent anywhere to be processed. The clinic’s system keeps a scan sent from a phone only for a patient whose consent is recorded. Each clinic’s data is kept apart, and the system logs who opened or changed a case. Obtaining consent and telling patients the purpose remain the clinic’s duties under PDPA.
