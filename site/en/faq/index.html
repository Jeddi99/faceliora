Know the difference first.

What we know now, and what still needs checking.

Simulation is not diagnosis, prediction or a guarantee of treatment outcomes.

Is DooDee web 3D?

No. It uses static 2D front, 45° and 90° images, not rotatable 3D.

Which product scans?

Faceliora scans on iPhone. DooDeeClinic uses supplied models on desktop. DooDee mobile is a separate personal app.

Which devices are supported?

Current iPhone and desktop requirements remain unverified. Legacy Windows/Mac copy does not prove installer availability. Check device and model-transfer compatibility.

Can simulation predict treatment results?

No. It supports discussion, not diagnosis, prediction or guaranteed outcomes. A doctor must assess treatment.

Is the 30-day trial available?

Thirty days is intended. Availability, accounts, activation, expiry and installers remain unconfirmed. This mockup starts no trial.

Where are downloads and demos?

Web: www.doodee.app. Mobile, scanner, desktop and demo destinations remain pending. No requests or patient data are collected here.
