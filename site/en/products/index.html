Essential information for clinics and clients.

How do Faceliora Clinic and Faceliora Web differ?

Faceliora Clinic is 3D facial simulation software for clinics, working on scans from the Faceliora Scan app or 3D files. Faceliora Web is a consumer product for facial analysis and 2D procedure simulation in front (0°) and side (90°) views.

Does Faceliora Clinic create its own 3D scans?

Faceliora Clinic does not scan. Scan the face with the Faceliora Scan app on an iPhone and send it to Faceliora Clinic, or import a .glb, .obj or .zip file from another scanning app. Confirm supported iPhone models with our team first.

Can Faceliora Web rotate a 3D model?

It uses 2D images in front (0°) and side (90°) views. It is not a rotatable 3D model.

Where is Faceliora Web available now?

On the web. Its mobile app is in development; we do not show App Store or Google Play download buttons yet.

Does simulation guarantee treatment results?

No. It helps explore and communicate preferences. A clinician must assess suitability, risks and possible outcomes.
