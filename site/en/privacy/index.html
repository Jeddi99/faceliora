Data controller

Faceliora team, operated by Mr. Jeddilok Sudnet, is the data controller of the personal data collected through this website.

Address: Mueang Ubon Ratchathani 34000, Thailand.

Privacy contact: privacy@faceliora.com. We do not appoint a data protection officer or an EU representative.

Data we collect

Clinic demo contact form: name, clinic or organisation, phone number, email and country, plus the details you choose to add: LINE ID, province (shown only when the country is Thailand), role, preferred day to be contacted, and a message. We also record the page and button you came from, the ad campaign values in the link you followed (utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign, utm_content), the site language, the version of the consent you gave and the time we received the form.

Email you send us: the address you write from and what you write.

Technical data: our hosting provider, Vercel, processes technical request information such as IP address, browser type and the page requested, to serve and secure this website. The website loads analytics (Google Analytics 4) and marketing (Meta Pixel) scripts only if you accept that cookie category. See “Cookies and browser storage”.

Face images and health data: this website does not collect them. Please do not send them (see “What not to send”).

Why we use it, and our legal bases

We use your data only for the purposes below.

  • To answer your demo request and arrange a demonstration. Legal basis: taking steps at your request before a possible contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b); contract basis under the PDPA).
  • To contact you back and keep your details, as you agreed when you ticked the consent box. Legal basis: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a); PDPA consent). You can withdraw it at any time.
  • To measure website use (Google Analytics 4) and the results of our ads (Meta Pixel). Legal basis: your consent given in the cookie banner (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a); PDPA consent). We do not load these tools unless you accept. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • To keep a record of business enquiries and to protect this website from spam and abuse. Legal basis: our legitimate interests (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f); PDPA legitimate interests). You can object to this use.
  • To meet a legal obligation where the law requires it. Legal basis: legal obligation.

Who handles your data

We use these service providers (processors) that handle data on our behalf:

  • Google (Google Sheets, and Gmail or Apps Script for notifications): stores the form entries and the emails we receive.
  • Vercel: hosts this website.
  • Google Analytics 4 (Google LLC): analyses website use, only if you accept the analytics cookie category.
  • Meta Pixel (Meta Platforms, Inc. and its affiliates): measures our Facebook and Instagram ads, only if you accept the marketing cookie category.

We do not sell personal data. We disclose data to authorities only where the law requires it.

Transfers outside your country

Google (including Google Analytics), Meta Platforms and Vercel store and process data on servers that can be outside your country, including outside Thailand and outside the EU, EEA and UK. Where the law requires safeguards for such transfers, we rely on the standard contractual clauses that Google and Meta rely on for their services.

How long we keep it

Form entries and emails are kept until 12 months after our last contact with you, unless you become a customer. Technical data held by Vercel follows Vercel’s own retention. Data in Google Analytics and Meta Pixel is kept for each provider’s default period (Google’s _ga cookie typically lasts about 2 years and Meta’s _fbp about 3 months, per the providers’ documentation). We do not configure longer retention.

Your rights

You may ask us to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you
  • correct it (rectification)
  • erase it
  • restrict how we use it
  • give it to you in a portable format
  • stop using it for the reasons in “legitimate interests” (objection)

You may also withdraw your consent at any time; this does not affect what we did before. Write to privacy@faceliora.com. We will answer within the time the law requires.

You may complain to the data protection supervisory authority in your country, or to Thailand’s Personal Data Protection Committee (PDPC).

Cookies and browser storage

We set analytics and marketing cookies only if you accept them. If you reject, or have not chosen, we do not load Google or Meta scripts and neither sets a cookie.

  • Essential (always on): your cookie choice is kept in your browser’s localStorage (fl_consent), and short-lived values in sessionStorage let the opening animation play once per visit and remember the ad campaign values (utm_*) in the link you followed so they can be sent with a form. sessionStorage is cleared when you close the tab. These values stay in your browser, are not cookies, and are not sent to us, except the campaign values sent with a form.
  • Analytics (only if you accept): Google Analytics 4, by Google LLC, sets cookies such as _ga and _ga_* to count visitors and the pages they open. We do not send your name, phone number or email to Google Analytics.
  • Marketing (only if you accept): Meta Pixel, by Meta Platforms, sets cookies such as _fbp to measure our ads on Facebook and Instagram. We send events such as “page view”, “form sent” or “contact click” without your name, phone number or email.

You can withdraw consent or change your choice at any time with the link at the bottom of every page. When you withdraw, we stop sending data to that provider and delete the _ga and _fbp cookies this website set, as far as your browser allows. You can also delete cookies in your browser settings.

Children

This website is for clinic professionals and is not directed at children. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, write to privacy@faceliora.com and we will delete it.

What not to send

Do not include face photos, health information or patient details, whether by email or in the form’s message field. Contact the same email to ask about information you have sent.

Facial data in our products

How clinic products store and use face scans is explained on the Faceliora Scan page and in the facial-data privacy guide.
