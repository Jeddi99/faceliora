Clinic demo contact form

When you fill in and send the clinic demo contact form, we use your details as follows.

Purpose: to contact you about the demo

Data we collect: name, clinic, phone number and email, plus the details you choose to add: LINE ID, province, role, preferred day to be contacted, and a message

Where it is stored: Google Sheets in the data controller’s Google account

How long we keep it: until 12 months after our last contact with you, unless you become a customer

Your rights: you may ask to access, correct or delete your data by emailing doodeeontop01@gmail.com

What not to send

Do not include face photos, health information or patient details, whether by email or in the form’s message field. Contact the same email to ask about information you have sent.
