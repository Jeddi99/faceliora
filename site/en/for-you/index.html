Preparing original photographs

Is Faceliora Web 3D?

No. The two views (front and side) are separate 2D images, not a rotatable 3D model. Simulation on a 3D facial scan is what Faceliora Clinic does, in clinics.

2During the consultation

Bring it to a consultation

Use the images to start a conversation, not replace a clinician.

Use simulations to communicate questions. Medical decisions remain with a doctor.

  • What important risks or limitations for me are not shown in this image?
  • What is a reasonable expectation, and what cannot be guaranteed?
10 questions to ask before an aesthetic treatment

What the simulation cannot show

  • Simulation is not diagnosis, prediction or a guarantee of treatment outcomes.
  • A visually adjusted face does not display procedural risks, bodily response or the experience of recovery.
  • Suitability, risks and possible outcomes must be assessed by a clinician.

Can I bring Faceliora Web images to a clinic?

Yes. Use them to start a conversation with a clinic and a clinician, not as a conclusion in place of an examination.

3After the consultation

Separate explanations from expectations in your notes

Afterwards, make three columns: what the clinician explained, your understanding, and questions still open. Date any accompanying images and label each as an original, reference or simulation.

  • What the clinician explained
  • Your understanding
  • Questions still open

Facial photographs and 3D models

Ask about the whole data journey, not just the final app.

  • Who can access the information, and how is access limited?
  • How long is each type retained, and why?
Privacy questions before sharing facial images

Does a Faceliora Web image show my treatment result?

No. A simulation helps explore and communicate preferences; it does not guarantee a result. Suitability, risks and possible outcomes must be assessed by a clinician.

Faceliora Web and Faceliora App

Faceliora personal use has separate web and mobile paths.

Open Faceliora Web

How is Faceliora Web different from Faceliora Clinic?

Faceliora Web is used on the web by anyone and works with 2D images. Faceliora Clinic is software for clinics that simulates injections, surgery and thread lifts on a 3D facial scan and summarises the consultation.

Neither product description establishes that the two products automatically exchange files, share accounts or use the same client workflow. Ask about any connection you need before planning it.
