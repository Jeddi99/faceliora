Faceliora at P1 New Regional Startup 2026

Activities / StartupUpdated 6 October 2026By Faceliora team

Faceliora shares a moment from its startup development journey at P1 New Regional Startup 2026, pictured at Ubon Ratchathani University Science Park.

A regional entrepreneurship programme

The event backdrop names P1 New Regional Startup 2026 and describes a programme for developing new entrepreneurs in the region. It identifies Startup, SMEs and community enterprises as part of that context.

A moment from Faceliora’s journey

The site owner supplied this image of participation in the startup activities to share the team’s story alongside its product development.
