From a phone model to Faceliora Clinic

News & eventsUpdated 2 October 2026By Faceliora team

Use a phone and an external app to create a 3D model, then import it into our Faceliora Clinic software.

Scanning and model creation happen in the external app. Confirm supported files, devices and import steps before starting.

In Faceliora Clinic, view and adjust the model to support a conversation with a clinic and clinician.

From a facial scan to a consultation sheet.

  1. Scan the face in 3D

    Scan with the Faceliora Scan app on an iPhone and send it to Faceliora Clinic, or import a GLB, OBJ or ZIP file from another scanning app.

  2. Open the case in Faceliora Clinic

    The software finds the key facial landmarks and draws the zones; the clinician can check and move any point.

  3. Simulate together

    Simulate injections, surgery or thread lifts, then view the simulated result from several angles.

  4. Summarise and consult a clinician

    Export the consultation sheet, with a clinician assessing suitability before any treatment decision.

Visualization supports a consultation. It does not guarantee treatment outcomes. Assessment and advice remain the physician’s responsibility.
