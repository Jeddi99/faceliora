What Faceliora Clinic does

  • Injection simulation

    Choose HA filler, botulinum toxin or fat and press the face where it goes.

  • Surgery simulation, 13 zones

    Adjusted with sliders in millimetres, on both sides or one.

  • Thread-lift simulation

    Jawline, under-chin, cheek, nasolabial fold, mouth corner, brow and eye-tail threads.

  • 3D skin analysis

    Shown in simulated daylight, red and UV views for a clinician’s review. Not a diagnosis.

  • Consultation summary and exports

    Before-and-after images from the 3D model, a comparison video and a PDF.

Capability details are as described by the previous website; not yet verified by product testing.
