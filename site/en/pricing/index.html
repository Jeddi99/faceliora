Work out whether it pays off for your clinic

Enter your clinic’s own numbers. The page calculates instantly and compares both plans: unlimited cases at ฿9,900, or 30 cases at ฿4,990, per month per clinic (1 case = 1 photo upload · 30-case plan: ฿100 per extra case). Consultation time is an optional extra: fill it in or skip it.

This calculator needs JavaScript

You can work it out by hand with the formula below. Nothing is sent anywhere.

  1. Extra cases = consultations × (expected close rate − current close rate)
  2. Extra revenue per month = extra cases × average case value
  3. Monthly fee: unlimited plan = ฿9,900 · 30-case plan = ฿4,990 + ฿100 × cases above 30 (cases = the cases field; if empty, consultations per month)
  4. Net after the fee = extra revenue − that plan’s monthly fee
  5. Break-even = the plan’s monthly fee ÷ average case value, rounded up to whole cases
  6. Minutes saved per month = consultations × (minutes today − expected minutes)
  7. Shown as hours = minutes ÷ 60 (under 60 minutes it is shown in minutes)
  8. Value of time = hours saved × cost of time per hour (only if you enter it)

Made-up example to show the method, not any clinic’s data

  • 40 people × (35% − 30%) = 2 cases
  • 2 cases × ฿50,000 = ฿100,000
  • If 40 cases are uploaded: unlimited ฿9,900 · 30-case ฿4,990 + ฿100 × 10 = ฿5,990
  • Unlimited: ฿100,000 − ฿9,900 = ฿90,100 · 30-case: ฿100,000 − ฿5,990 = ฿94,010
  • Break-even ฿9,900 ÷ ฿50,000 = 0.2, rounded up to 1 case (฿5,990 ÷ ฿50,000 ≈ 0.1 is also 1 case)
  • Time (made up): 40 people × (50 − 35 min) = 600 min = 10 hours

These figures are an estimate based on what you enter. They do not guarantee revenue, time saved or treatment results.
