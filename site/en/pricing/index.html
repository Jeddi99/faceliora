Work out whether it pays off for your clinic

Enter your clinic’s own numbers. The page calculates instantly. Enter your Faceliora monthly fee from your quote to see break-even and the net after the fee. Consultation time is an optional extra: fill it in or skip it.

This calculator needs JavaScript

You can work it out by hand with the formula below. Nothing is sent anywhere.

  1. Extra cases = consultations × (expected close rate − current close rate)
  2. Extra revenue per month = extra cases × average case value
  3. Monthly fee = the number your clinic enters in the Faceliora monthly fee field, from your quote (optional; if empty, break-even and the net after the fee are not shown)
  4. Net after the fee = extra revenue − the monthly fee you entered
  5. Break-even = the monthly fee you entered ÷ average case value, rounded up to whole cases
  6. Minutes saved per month = consultations × (minutes today − expected minutes)
  7. Shown as hours = minutes ÷ 60 (under 60 minutes it is shown in minutes)
  8. Value of time = hours saved × cost of time per hour (only if you enter it)

Made-up example to show the method, not any clinic’s data

  • 40 people × (35% − 30%) = 2 cases
  • 2 cases × 50,000 baht = 100,000 baht
  • Net after the fee = 100,000 baht − the fee the clinic enters from its quote
  • Break-even = the fee entered ÷ 50,000, rounded up to whole cases
  • Time (made up): 40 people × (50 − 35 min) = 600 min = 10 hours

These figures are an estimate based on what you enter. They do not guarantee revenue, time saved or treatment results.

How it is calculated

  1. Extra cases = consultations × (expected close rate − current close rate)
  2. Extra revenue per month = extra cases × average case value
  3. Monthly fee = the number your clinic enters in the Faceliora monthly fee field, from your quote (optional; if empty, break-even and the net after the fee are not shown)
  4. Net after the fee = extra revenue − the monthly fee you entered
  5. Break-even = the monthly fee you entered ÷ average case value, rounded up to whole cases
  6. Minutes saved per month = consultations × (minutes today − expected minutes)
  7. Shown as hours = minutes ÷ 60 (under 60 minutes it is shown in minutes)
  8. Value of time = hours saved × cost of time per hour (only if you enter it)

This is revenue, not profit: your clinic’s own costs are not deducted. Extra cases and hours show one decimal; money is rounded to the baht.

If the expected minutes are not lower than today’s, it says “No time saved” and never shows a negative figure. Value of time is not revenue and is not part of “After the fee”.
