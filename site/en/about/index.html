About us

Faceliora makes Faceliora Clinic, 3D facial simulation software for aesthetic clinics, and Faceliora Web, web-based 2D facial analysis, to support clinician-led consultations.

Connecting technology and care.
Making understanding the starting point.

We believe a clearer view helps people communicate their preferences. Our technology supports exploration and consultation, never replacing a clinician’s assessment or advice.

Better care starts
with better understanding.

Faceliora develops tools for a clearer conversation about facial preferences: 3D software for clinics and 2D facial analysis and simulation for individuals.

Faceliora Clinic and Faceliora Web have distinct workflows. We make each product’s role and availability clear.
